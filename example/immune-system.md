---
title: "The Immune System as Pattern Recognition"
date: "2025-05-15"
---
Your immune system solves a brutal classification problem: distinguish self from a vast, shifting space of pathogens it has never seen. The innate arm recognizes broad danger signatures fast; the adaptive arm generates enormous antibody diversity by randomly recombining gene segments, then selects and amplifies whatever binds the invader. It even keeps a memory of past infections for faster future response. The failure modes are illuminating — autoimmunity is misclassification of self, allergy is overreaction to the harmless, and cancer partly a failure to flag corrupted self.
