---
title: "The Grammar of Jazz Improvisation"
date: "2025-03-18"
---
Improvisation looks like pure spontaneity but rests on deep structure. A soloist navigates a repeating chord progression in real time, choosing notes that imply the underlying harmony while bending against it for tension. Years of practicing scales and licks build a vocabulary that becomes automatic, freeing attention for phrasing and conversation with the rest of the band. The paradox is that the most free-sounding playing is the most thoroughly prepared — constraint is what makes the freedom legible. It is composition at the speed of performance.
