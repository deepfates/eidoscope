title: "Reinforcement Learning's Core Loop"
date: "2025-06-01"
---
Reinforcement learning studies agents that learn by acting: try something, observe a reward, adjust to get more reward next time. Unlike supervised learning there is no labeled answer, only a delayed and often sparse signal, which makes credit assignment — figuring out which past action caused a later reward — the central difficulty. The field swings between elegant value-based methods, policy gradients, and model-based planning. Its recent fame comes from pairing it with deep networks and, lately, using it to fine-tune language models against human preferences, where the "environment" is a conversation.
