---
title: "Why Rome Fell (It's Complicated)"
date: "2025-05-02"
---
The fall of the Western Roman Empire resists single causes. Fiscal overreach, currency debasement, plague, climate shifts, overextended frontiers, and migrating peoples under pressure from further east all compounded over centuries. The empire did not so much collapse as fragment and transform, its institutions decaying unevenly while the Eastern half endured another thousand years. The lesson historians keep relearning is that complex systems fail through the interaction of many stresses, not one dramatic blow — and that "decline" is often only obvious in retrospect.
