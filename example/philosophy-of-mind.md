---
title: "The Hard Problem of Consciousness"
date: "2025-02-22"
---
We can explain, in principle, how the brain processes information, controls behavior, and reports on its own states. What we cannot explain is why any of it is accompanied by subjective experience — why there is something it is like to see red. Chalmers named this the hard problem, distinguishing it from the merely difficult "easy" problems of cognition. Responses range from illusionism (experience is a useful fiction) to panpsychism (experience is fundamental). The discomfort is that our best physical theories describe structure and function, and experience seems to be neither.
