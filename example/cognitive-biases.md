---
title: "Why Cognitive Biases Persist"
date: "2025-04-19"
---
Human judgment departs systematically from the prescriptions of logic and probability — we anchor on irrelevant numbers, overweight vivid risks, and see patterns in noise. Kahneman and Tversky catalogued these not as random errors but as the shadows of fast, frugal heuristics that usually work. The biases persist because the heuristics were adaptive in environments unlike spreadsheets and casinos. Debiasing is hard precisely because the shortcuts are not bolted on; they are how cognition runs by default. Knowing about a bias rarely dissolves it.
